Privacy Policy

App: Eisenhower — Priority Matrix  ·  Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Cédric SUSLEC ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application Eisenhower — Priority Matrix ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

The App collects and processes the following data:

  • Task data: Tasks and settings you create are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA/GAID): With your permission, we use your device's advertising identifier to serve personalized ads through Google AdMob.
  • Anonymous analytics: We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases. RevenueCat may collect anonymized purchase event data.
  • Crash reports: If the app crashes, anonymized diagnostic data may be collected by the platform (Apple/Google) to help us improve stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display relevant advertising (Google AdMob)
  • To process and restore in-app purchases (RevenueCat)
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchases

4. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, we request permission to track your activity via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing your advertising identifier. You can deny this permission and the App will continue to function with non-personalized ads.

5. Data Retention

Your task data is stored locally on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App. We do not retain personal data on our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
